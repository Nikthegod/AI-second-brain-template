# Index Funds vs Active Funds

## Summary
- **Index funds** aim to match a market index by holding its components; **active funds** pay managers to try to beat it.
- Index funds usually charge lower fees, and fees compound against returns over long periods.
- Active funds can outperform in a given period, but consistently beating the index after fees is difficult.

## Key points
- Fee differences look small per year but grow large over decades ([example source](https://example.com)).
- Diversification: a broad index fund spreads risk across many companies ([example source](https://example.com)).
