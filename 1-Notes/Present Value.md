# Present Value

- **In plain words:** what a future payment is worth in today's money.
- **Formula:** $PV = \dfrac{FV}{(1+r)^n}$ — future value, discounted at rate $r$ over $n$ periods.
- **Why it matters:** the building block of every valuation — bonds, projects, and whole companies are the present value of their future cash flows.
