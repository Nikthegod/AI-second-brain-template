# Compound Interest

- **In plain words:** earning returns on your past returns, so growth speeds up over time.
- **Formula:** $FV = PV \times (1+r)^n$
- **Example:** $1,000 at 7% a year grows to about $1,967 after 10 years ($1.07^{10} \approx 1.967$), and to about $7,612 after 30 years ($1.07^{30} \approx 7.612$) — time does most of the work.
- **The flip side:** fees compound too. A yearly fee is subtracted from the return every year, so its cost grows the same way.
